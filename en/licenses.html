Licenses

Earshot stands on the shoulders of many open source projects. Thank you to everyone who works on them. The complete list with all license texts is in the app under Settings → About Earshot.

Key building blocks

  • whisper.cpp (MIT) for speech recognition
  • llama.cpp (MIT) for the language model
  • SQLCipher (BSD-3-Clause) for encrypted storage
  • Tauri (MIT/Apache-2.0) as the app framework
  • React (MIT) for the interface

Models

The app downloads the models when needed; they are not part of the installer.

  • OpenAI Whisper in ggml format (MIT)
  • Qwen3 in GGUF format (Apache-2.0)
