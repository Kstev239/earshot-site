4. The Earshot app

  • Audio, captions, summaries and notes are processed only on your computer. I receive none of it; there is no account and no server.
  • Audio is never stored. Earshot only stores text when you save a meeting, and then encrypted on your computer. You can delete everything in the app.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash reporting that sends data to me.

The app only connects to the internet in these cases:

  • Downloading speech models (when you start it): from Hugging Face, Inc., USA (huggingface.co). Hugging Face sees your IP address and which file is downloaded.
  • Checking for updates (once a day, never during a meeting, can be turned off): a request to GitHub, see section 2. No data about you or your meetings is sent.
