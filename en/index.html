Features

Stay in the loop without listening all the time

Captions that follow you

Captions appear on the screen your mouse is on, unobtrusive and click-through. Keep working in your editor, browser or spreadsheet.

Your name comes up? You’ll know.

Earshot highlights your name, and ⌥⌘R or Ctrl+Alt+R shows you in seconds what was just discussed and what you were asked.

Notes that write themselves

After the meeting: summary, decisions and tasks with due dates. Searchable, sorted by project, and you can simply ask your meetings.

100 % local

No bot in your meeting. No cloud.

Speech recognition and the language model run on your computer. That’s why Earshot also works where cloud note-taking bots are banned.

Conversations stay on your computer

Audio and text are never sent anywhere. There is no account and no server.

No audio recording

Earshot never stores audio. Text only goes to disk when you save a meeting.

Stored encrypted

Saved meetings live in an encrypted database, with the key in your system’s keychain.

Nobody in the meeting notices

Earshot listens to your system sound. It doesn’t join meetings and needs no permission from the host.

Get started

Three steps to your first caption

  1. Install

    Download and open Earshot. On a Mac, you allow screen and system audio recording once.

  2. Load a speech model

    Once, about 0.2 to 5 GB depending on the model. Earshot recommends the right one for your computer.

  3. Start a meeting

    From the menu bar icon or with ⌥⌘M / Ctrl+Alt+M. Captions appear after about a second.

FAQ

Good to know

Which meeting apps does Earshot work with?

Any app that plays sound on your computer: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Slack huddles, Discord and even videos in your browser. Earshot listens to your system sound, not to one specific app.

Do I have to tell the other participants?

Earshot doesn’t record audio and only stores text if you want it to. Still, it’s fair, and common in many companies, to let people know. Earshot gives you a ready-made note to copy. What applies to you legally depends on your country and your employer.

Does Earshot need the internet?

Only to download the speech models once and to check for updates. During a meeting, Earshot makes no connections. The content of your meetings never leaves your computer.

Which languages does Earshot understand?

Earshot is made for German and English. In the settings you can also choose automatic language detection, which understands more languages but is less reliable. The app itself is available in German and English.

How much does Earshot cost?

Nothing during the beta. Afterwards, live captions and the alert when your name comes up stay free. Unlimited recaps, summaries and your meeting memory will be part of a Pro subscription.
