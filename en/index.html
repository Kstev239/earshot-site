100 % local

No bot in the meeting. No cloud. Check it yourself.

Speech recognition and the language model run on your computer, even offline. Even I, as the developer, can’t get to your meetings.

  • No audio on disk Earshot listens to your system sound and records nothing.
  • Only what you save is saved After the meeting it’s your call. If you don’t save, the transcript is gone once you quit the app.
  • Encrypted Saved meetings live in an encrypted database, with the key in your system’s keychain.
  • No account, no tracking There is no server Earshot could send anything about you to.

Get started

Three steps to your first caption

  1. Install

    Download, open, done. On a Mac you allow system audio recording once. On Windows you don’t need admin rights.

  2. Load a speech model

    Once, 0.2 to 5 GB depending on the model. Earshot quickly measures what your computer can handle and suggests the right one.

  3. Start a meeting

    ⌥⌘M or Ctrl+Alt+M. About a second after someone finishes a sentence, it’s on your screen.

FAQ

What people ask first

Which meeting apps does it work with?

Anything that plays sound on your computer: Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Slack huddles, Discord, even a video in your browser. Earshot listens to the system sound, not to one particular app.

Do I have to tell the others?

Earshot doesn’t record audio and only stores text if you want it to. Telling people is still fair and common in many companies. The app has a sentence you can copy for that. What applies legally depends on your country and your employer.

How good is the recognition?

Earshot uses Whisper, a widely used speech recognition model, right on your computer. With clear speech the captions are very accurate. A bad microphone or lots of jargon cause mistakes, as with any speech recognition.

Which languages?

German and English. In the settings you can also choose automatic language detection, which understands more languages but is less reliable.

Will it slow down my computer?

During a meeting the speech recognition is busy, which you’ll notice in the fan before you notice it in speed. Without a meeting Earshot does practically nothing. 16 GB of memory makes summaries and recaps comfortable.

How much does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. Afterwards, captions and the alert for your name stay free. Unlimited recaps, summaries and your meeting memory go into a Pro subscription.

Email Kelmsteven8@gmail.com. Earshot is built by one person, so every message gets read.
